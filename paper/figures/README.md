# Paper figures

These figures are the authoritative plots used by `../main.tex`.

The corrected comparison uses `integrated_accuracy_curves`, `integrated_final_comparison`, `integrated_factorial`, `integrated_communication`, and `integrated_rank_trajectories`, each in PDF and PNG. They are generated on gpu003 by `scripts/summarize_integrated.py` from all 27 raw records in `docs/artifacts/integrated-corrected/`. `summary.json` records input, script, and figure hashes. Error bars are sample standard deviations over seeds 42--44; the communication plot includes control traffic and one final assembly. The following figures retain their historical protocols.

- `p3_uniform_curves.png` and `p3_uniform_domains.png` come from the completed
  remote report for context `ded87b4e7797` (rank 16, 50 rounds, seeds 42--44).
- `p3_heterogeneous_curves.png` and `p3_heterogeneous_domains.png` come from
  context `b729ad24b6b0` (ranks 4/12/32, 50 rounds, seeds 42--44), including
  the delta-W, factor-zero-padding, and feedback arms where applicable.
- `p3_signature_ari.png` is generated from
  `docs/artifacts/p3-signatures/summary.csv`, aggregating mean and sample SD by
  training stage, method, and signature.
- `p2_lambda_validation.png` is generated from the regenerated
  `docs/artifacts/p2-exp2-real-seed42/evaluation_metrics.csv` global rows.

The underlying aggregate CSV/JSON files and manifests are retained under
`docs/artifacts/` so the plotted values can be audited.

The completed methodology exploration adds five PDF/PNG pairs generated on
gpu003 from the archived run records:

- `exploration_optimization`: seven paired three-seed optimization/ownership
  controls; original 50,000 train / 10,000 official-test examples. Generated by
  `scripts/summarize_methodology_exploration.py`.
- `exploration_rank_retention`: no-training stress test from completed pooled
  checkpoints; initial per-client projection and 30 mixing/projection cycles.
  Generated by `experiments/diagnose_rank_retention.py` in Project 3.
- `exploration_gradient_equivalence`: the exact full-data synchronized-gradient
  control, with matching predictions at all 90 epoch evaluations. Sources and
  plot script are under `methodology-exploration/gradient-equivalence/`.
- `exploration_residual`: all eleven endpoints in the seed42 retained-base
  screen, including the unsuccessful eight residual recipes and finite
  high-gain loss growth. Generated by
  `scripts/summarize_residual_exploration.py` from `residual-screen-v1/`.
- `exploration_masked`: final accuracy and curves for four paired three-seed
  partial-gradient/pooled arms. Generated by
  `scripts/summarize_masked_exploration.py` from the six validation run
  directories documented in `methodology-exploration/README.md`.

The residual and masked figures use the same 45,000 fitting / 5,000 validation
split of the original training set and never open the official test tensor.
The residual screen has one seed and no uncertainty bars. Masked error bars and
bands are sample SD over seeds 42--44, not confidence intervals. Its uniform
control has identical final predictions to pooled LoRA at all three seeds but
matches intermediate correct counts in 89/90 evaluations, distinct from the
earlier exact full-data control. The small gradient-coordinate budgets in this
figure do not reduce full rank-16 model or optimizer storage.
